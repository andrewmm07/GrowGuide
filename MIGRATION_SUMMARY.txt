WHAT CHANGES IN DATABASE
================================================================================

TABLE: plant_timelines
  BEFORE: plant_name UNIQUE, au_hardiness_zone UNIQUE (blocks same plant/zone)
  AFTER:  UNIQUE(plant_name, au_hardiness_zone) composite

TABLE: plant_activities (NEW)
  Columns: id, plant_name, au_hardiness_zone, timing_days, activity_name,
           details, activity_category, created_at
  FK: References plant_timelines(plant_name, au_hardiness_zone)
  Indexes: On (plant_name, au_hardiness_zone), (activity_category), (timing_days)
  Data: 3500+ rows (parsed from key_activities JSON)

TABLE: user_plants (DELETED)
  Reason: Never used in codebase
  Status: Dropped entirely

TABLE: garden_plants
  REMOVED: activity_type, type, schedule, full_schedule, is_harvested
  ADDED: au_hardiness_zone, completed_activities (UUID[]), last_activity_date
